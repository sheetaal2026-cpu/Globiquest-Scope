Active backdrop: plain muted slate stage; no photographic panorama.

Original procedural city landmarks, telescope, travel-star navigation, word crystal and artist island made for Atlas. Telescope imagery is a live render of the app globe, not a satellite feed or astronomical data.

Cafe rehearsal: original scripted practice phrases in English, Japanese, French, Spanish and Hindi. Input uses selectable replies; it does not assess speech pronunciation. Scores reflect hints and retries within this activity.

Artwork and passport/cafe progress save in browser storage. PNG export happens only when selected by the user.

Music: original synthesized regional sketches; instrument-inspired themes, not recordings of traditional performances.
