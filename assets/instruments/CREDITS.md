# Recorded instrument samples

CC0 samples from [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) and [VCSL](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), by Versilian Studios and contributors. Upright piano sampled by Simon Dalzell / Ivy Audio. Source URLs are listed in SOURCES.json. Full licenses are included. Samples were trimmed, converted to mono MP3 and peak-normalized. Guitar uses original plucked-string synthesis.
